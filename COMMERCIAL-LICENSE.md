## Do I need a commercial license?

The AGPL lets you use, modify and run lti-moodle-integration for free, **on
condition that the application you use it in is also released as open source
under the AGPL**. This module runs inside your server, and the AGPL's network
clause covers software that users reach over a network, such as a tool
launched from Moodle. You must therefore offer those users the complete
source code of your application.

| Your situation | License |
|---|---|
| Open-source LTI tool whose source is published under the AGPL | AGPL, free |
| Personal project, research or teaching, with source published under the AGPL | AGPL, free |
| Commercial LTI tool, SaaS or internal app whose source you don't publish | **Commercial** |
| Client work, or institution-wide deployment of a closed-source tool | **Commercial** |
| Evaluating or prototyping, not yet in production | AGPL, free |

If you're unsure, ask. We're happy to help.
